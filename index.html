One Piece Manga: Story, Anime, Characters and Ratings

One Piece is the long-running Japanese adventure manga written and illustrated by Eiichiro Oda. It follows Monkey D. Luffy, a determined young pirate whose rubber-like body and fearless spirit help him build a crew and pursue the legendary treasure known as the One Piece. The series combines exploration, comedy, emotional character stories, mysteries, and large-scale battles.

Story and world

After being inspired by the pirate Red-Haired Shanks, Luffy sets out to become King of the Pirates. He gathers the Straw Hat Pirates, including Roronoa Zoro, Nami, Usopp, Sanji, Tony Tony Chopper, Nico Robin, Franky, Brook, and Jinbe. Their journey crosses unusual islands and powerful nations, gradually revealing secrets about the world’s history, the World Government, and the meaning of freedom. The story is known for long-running mysteries, foreshadowing, and arcs that connect personal struggles to wider political conflicts.

Genre, author and publication

Author and artist: Eiichiro Oda. Genres: action, adventure, fantasy, comedy and drama. Original publication: Weekly Shōnen Jump. The manga remains ongoing; chapter availability and release timing can change, so check this page for entries added by the site administrator.

Does One Piece have an anime?

Yes. The television anime adaptation produced by Toei Animation began airing in 1999 and has continued through many story arcs. There are also theatrical films, specials and other related projects. The anime adapts much of the manga but may use different pacing, added scenes, and anime-original material. If you want the original sequence of events and the fullest story detail, the manga is a useful companion to the anime.

One Piece ratings

Community scores vary by platform and update as viewers vote. The long-running TV anime is generally rated highly by anime communities; check the current One Piece listing on MyAnimeList or another rating service for its live score rather than treating any number as permanent. Manga ratings also differ by platform and by whether users rate the whole series or individual volumes.

Why readers follow One Piece

Readers often praise the imaginative island settings, distinct crew personalities, emotional backstories, humor, and carefully planted mysteries. Its scale makes it rewarding for long-term readers, while each major island adventure introduces its own conflicts and supporting cast. New readers should expect a very long series and can start from Chapter 1 for the complete intended experience.
